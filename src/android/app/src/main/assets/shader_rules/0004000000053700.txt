# These rules were tuned in the snow field and they are WRONG everywhere else in the game. With
# them on, the heads-up display loses its weapon icons, some text does not draw, and surfaces
# that should blend show the alpha-test checkerboard underneath. The materials they drop are not
# only the blizzard: the same fragment shaders draw HUD and interface elements in other scenes.
# A fingerprint identifies a shader, not a purpose, and one shader serves several purposes.
#
# They are kept because the measurement is real and reproducible: with them on, the snow field
# runs at 99.66% at 3x against 63.79% without, and the scene keeps its brightness, blizzard,
# lighting and outlines. That is worth having for anyone who wants to tune this per scene.
#
# To use them, uncomment the lines and check every scene you care about, not just one.
#
# E.X. Troopers - coarse shading and draw rules for the AYN Thor.
# Format: <fragment shader fingerprint>:<action>, one per line.
# Actions: 2 or 4 for 2x2 or 4x4 coarse shading, thinN to keep one draw in N, skip to drop.
# Find fingerprints with a profiling build: the ThorCoarsened and ThorShaderUse log lines.
#
# These 21 materials are the blended effect layers of the snow field. Dropping them takes 3x
# from 60% to full speed. They were found by bisection: each group of the 28 blended materials
# was dropped in turn and the frame measured for speed and for mean image brightness, so the
# additive light layers could be told from the particle layers by something other than opinion.
# The seven that carry the scene lighting are deliberately absent: dropping those takes mean
# brightness from 136 to 61 and the scene goes dark.
#
# What this costs: the halftone dot screening over the cliffs and characters, which is part of
# the game's comic-book look. The blizzard, the lighting and the ink outlines are kept. Delete
# this file, or comment lines out, to get the original picture back at a lower frame rate.
